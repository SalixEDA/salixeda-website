# Inserting nets into the schematic

## Enabling net insertion mode
To enable net insertion mode into the schematic, click this button.

![Net insertion mode](shot-2-stp-1-en.png)

## Entering nets
A net is simply a polyline that connects component pins. The start, each
vertex, and the end of the polyline are entered with the left mouse button. To interrupt polyline input,
press the right mouse button.

Net input mode is equipped with a "smart mode". This mode suggests a complete net if
net input has not yet started, or suggests completing the net if its input has started. You can
accept the suggestion by pressing the middle mouse button, or continue entering the net
manually and ignore the suggestion.

## Manual net entry
In cases where the schematic configuration does not allow the "smart mode" to determine a complete
net, you should start entering manually. Hover the mouse over a capacitor pin and press the left
mouse button to start entering a polyline. Move the mouse to the next vertex
and press the left mouse button to fix the vertex. Move the mouse towards
the terminating net pin and smart mode will suggest completing the net.
